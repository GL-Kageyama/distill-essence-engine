# Emblem（emblem）

- **Purpose**: Symbol ／ **Granularity×time**: one symbol × convolution ／ **Size & aspect**: square / circular
- **Summary**: A symmetric emblem enclosed in a circular frame, with meaning gathered at a single point in the center.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`＝symbol, `MOTTO`＝lettering at the bottom (optional)

## Composition grammar
A circular frame, symmetry, heraldic composition (shield, star, leaves), a ribbon below, one point at the center

## do
- Preserve the symmetry
- Raise a single point at the center

## avoid
- Asymmetry, multiple focal points, realism

## Prompt template
```text
A circular emblem of {SUBJECT}. Symmetric heraldic composition — shield, star, leaves —
within a circular frame, a single focal point at center, a ribbon below with {MOTTO}.
```
