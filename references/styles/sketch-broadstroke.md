# Sketch (broad stroke)（sketch-broadstroke）

- **Medium**: Hand-drawn ／ **Lineage**: Sketch ／ **Era**: —
- **Summary**: A single thick stroke suggests rather than fills in.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Broad strokes only (black ink or pencil; no fine outlines, no digital fill)
- Quick single strokes, translucent overlaps
- Unevenness, streaks, breaks, imperfect edges
- Strokes that go beyond the silhouette
- Suggest rather than fill in, leaving large areas undrawn (abstract up close, the subject resolving at a distance)

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: Subject at center, the surroundings left largely undrawn
- **Typography**: No text
- **Color**: Monochrome (black ink or pencil)
- **Texture & light**: Paper grain, brush unevenness, no shadows

## do
- Suggest with a single thick stroke, leave undrawn areas
- Translucent overlaps and breaks

## avoid
- Fine outlines, careful filling, black-ink contours, photographic realism

## Negative
`no fine outline, no careful fill, no black ink, no photorealism`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
A broad-stroke sketch of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}, with {ACCENT}.
Thick single strokes only, no fine outline, no filled-in color,
quick gestural marks, transparent overlapping, uneven broken edges,
large unfinished areas that read abstract up close but resolve at a distance.
```
