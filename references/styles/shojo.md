# Shojo manga（shojo）

- **Medium**: Hand-drawn ／ **Lineage**: Shojo manga ／ **Era**: Contemporary
- **Summary**: Fine delicate lines, large glossy eyes, petals and sparkles, soft pastel tones — emotion made gentle and decorative.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Fine, delicate ink lines (no heavy outlines)
- Large expressive eyes with layered highlights
- Floral and sparkle ornaments (petals, stars, shimmer)
- Soft pastel palette
- Emotion through symbols: concentration lines, floating flowers, a gentle blush
- Decorative panel borders and flowing hand-lettering

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: face and expression close-ups, ornamental negative space
- **Typography**: flowing hand-lettering, softly shaped speech bubbles
- **Color**: soft pastel, a single stronger accent
- **Texture & light**: light dot/soft screentone, gentle luminous light

## do
- Draw delicate lines and large glossy eyes
- Use pastel tones and flower/sparkle ornaments
- Show emotion through symbols

## avoid
- Heavy outlines, dark gritty tones, realism, crowded action

## Negative
`not photorealistic, no heavy ink, no dark gritty tone, no 3D render`
