# Oil painting（oil-painting）

- **Medium**: Hand-drawn ／ **Lineage**: Oil painting (classical to modern) ／ **Era**: —
- **Summary**: Thick impasto and strong light-and-dark fix the weight and time of oil on canvas.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Thick brushwork (impasto)
- Raised pigment
- Canvas weave
- Chiaroscuro
- Craquelure, translucent glaze layers
- The hardness of strokes left to dry

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: Chiaroscuro light and dark
- **Typography**: No text
- **Color**: Oil deepened through glazes
- **Texture & light**: Impasto, weave, craquelure, strong light and dark

## do
- Impasto and glazes, light and dark
- Keep the canvas and craquelure

## avoid
- Digital, flat color, airbrush smoothness

## Negative
`not digital, no flat color, no airbrush smoothness`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
An oil painting of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}, with {ACCENT}. Thick impasto
brushstrokes, raised pigment, canvas weave, chiaroscuro, fine craquelure, translucent
glaze layers, dry hardened strokes.
```
