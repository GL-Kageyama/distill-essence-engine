# 1970s New Hollywood（new-hollywood）

- **Medium**: Photography / film ／ **Lineage**: New Hollywood ／ **Era**: 1970s
- **Summary**: Grain, available light, and faded color — the honest cinema of the 1970s.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `LIGHT`＝available light

## Fidelity anchors
- Heavy film grain, 35mm celluloid texture
- Available light (windows, dusk, neon, streetlight) — not studio-polished
- Muted, faded color palette with a soft contrast
- Slight handheld or zoom-lens feel, imperfect framing
- 1970s production design: costumes, cars, interiors
- Naturalistic performance, ordinary settings

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: candid and human, telephoto compression or close framing
- **Typography**: no text
- **Color**: faded amber, olive, and brown; low saturation
- **Texture & light**: grain, natural window or street light, soft shadows

## do
- Light with the available environment, not the studio
- Keep the grain and the faded palette

## avoid
- Saturated modern color, clean digital sharpness, glossy studio lighting, CGI

## Negative
`no saturated color, no clean digital look, no glossy studio lighting, no CGI, no illustration`

A 1970s New Hollywood film still of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}, lit by
