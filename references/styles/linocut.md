# Linocut（linocut）

- **Medium**: Printmaking / print ／ **Lineage**: Relief printmaking ／ **Era**: Modern
- **Summary**: Gouge-carved marks and thick planes print a bold flat world in two colors.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Gouge-carved marks
- Thick planes and white cut lines
- Paper embossed by the block
- Two bold flat colors
- Ink unevenness, rough outlines

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: Thick planes and cut-out whites, rough outlines
- **Typography**: Hand-carved lettering in the block when needed
- **Color**: Two flat colors
- **Texture & light**: Paper embossed by the block, ink unevenness, no shadows

## do
- Thick planes and white cut lines, restrict to two colors
- Keep the embossing and ink unevenness

## avoid
- Fine gradients, photographic realism, digital

## Negative
`no fine gradient, no photorealistic, no digital`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
A linocut print of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}. Gouge-carved marks, thick flat areas
with white cut lines, two bold flat colors, ink unevenness, rough outlines, paper embossed
by the block.
```
