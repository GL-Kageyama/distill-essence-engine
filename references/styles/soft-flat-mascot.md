# Soft flat mascot（soft-flat-mascot）

- **Medium**: Digital ／ **Lineage**: Japanese transit-card / municipal mascot ／ **Era**: Contemporary
- **Summary**: Soft rounded shapes, matte flat color, a minimal thin outline and big simple eyes — the gentle character-branding style of Japanese transit-card mascots, rounder and more reserved than flat-comic.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- A soft, rounded, plump silhouette — no sharp corners, no spiky fur or feathers
- Matte flat color fills (no gradients, no gloss, no specular highlights)
- A single minimal thin outline around the whole figure
- Big simple eyes, a tiny or absent nose and mouth
- A restrained, simple face — expression over detail
- 2–4 flat colors, one brand accent color
- Kawaii proportions (large head, small body), slightly reserved rather than exaggerated
- A plain or single-color background

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: Subject centered, a plain background, generous negative space
- **Typography**: No text on the mascot itself (a single short caption or label is the format's call, not the style's)
- **Color**: 2–4 matte flat colors plus one brand accent; no gradients
- **Texture & light**: Flat and shadowless (a minimal grounding contact shadow is allowed only inside a photoreal composite)

## do
- Keep the silhouette round and plump
- Use matte flat color and a single minimal outline
- Big simple eyes, a restrained expression
- Leave the background plain

## avoid
- Gradients, gloss or specular highlights, realistic fur or texture, a 3D render, detailed facial features, a busy background, text on the mascot itself

## Negative
`not photorealistic, no 3D render, no gradient, no gloss or specular highlight, no realistic texture, no heavy shading`
