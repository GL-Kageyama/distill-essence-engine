# Landscape photography（landscape-photo）

- **Medium**: Photography ／ **Lineage**: Large-format landscape ／ **Era**: modern (f/64 school)
- **Summary**: Deep focus and the full tonal range render the land with quiet majesty.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`＝the land / scenery, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`＝place, `LIGHT`＝light, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- f/64 deep focus, everything in focus from foreground to horizon
- Full tonal range from deep shadow to bright highlight (zone system)
- Large-format fine detail, sharp edges
- Majestic scale, land and sky as the subject
- No human figure, no intrusion

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: wide and expansive, strong foreground framing, horizon in a third
- **Typography**: no text
- **Color**: natural film color, subtle warm or cool cast
- **Texture & light**: fine grain, direct sun or dramatic sky, crisp edges

## do
- Keep everything sharp from foreground to far distance
- Let the land and sky carry the frame alone

## avoid
- Shallow depth of field, HDR oversaturation, illustrated sky, people in frame

## Negative
`no shallow depth of field, no HDR oversaturation, no people, no CGI, no illustration`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
A large-format landscape photograph of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}, under
{LIGHT}. f/64 deep focus, full tonal range from deep shadow to bright highlight,
fine detail at every scale, unbroken depth from near ground to far horizon,
land and sky as the subject, natural film color.
```
