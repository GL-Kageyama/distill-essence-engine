# Swiss / International Typographic（swiss-style）

- **Medium**: Print / typographic ／ **Lineage**: Swiss / International Typographic ／ **Era**: 1950s–60s, still current
- **Summary**: A modular grid, flush-left ragged-right grotesque sans, red + black + white — typography as a visible system.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`＝the subject (a product, event, or message), `HEADLINE`＝the headline, `TAGLINE`＝the supporting line, `ACCENT`＝the single accent color

## Fidelity anchors
- A strict **modular grid**; **flush-left ragged-right** setting (never justified, never centered)
- One grotesque sans (Helvetica / Akzidenz family), a strong typographic scale
- **Red + black + white** as the default palette, with at most one accent
- Objective photography or flat color fields used as graphic material
- Typography is the design; the layout makes the grid visible

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: a strict modular grid, asymmetric balance, generous white space
- **Typography**: one grotesque sans, flush-left ragged-right, large scale contrasts
- **Color**: white ground, black text, red accent (or one accent only)
- **Texture / lighting**: flat, no texture, no ornament

## do
- Work a strict grid and make it visible
- Keep flush-left ragged-right, one typeface, strong scale
- Limit the palette to red + black + white plus at most one accent

## avoid
- Decorative ornaments, centered or justified setting, more than one typeface, pastel or multi-color palettes, skeuomorphic texture

## Negative
`not decorative, no ornaments, no centered or justified text, no multiple typefaces, no pastel or multi-color palette`

## Prompt template
```text
A Swiss-style graphic for {SUBJECT}, on a strict modular grid. {HEADLINE} in a large
grotesque sans (Helvetica / Akzidenz family), flush-left ragged-right, with {TAGLINE}
smaller and aligned to the grid. Red + black + white with one accent {ACCENT}.
Typography is the design — the grid stays visible, generous white space, flat, no
ornament.
```
