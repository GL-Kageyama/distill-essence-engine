# Pencil（pencil）

- **Medium**: Hand-drawn ／ **Lineage**: Drawing ／ **Era**: —
- **Summary**: Graphite value and hatching sharpen form into the paper grain.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Graphite value range
- Direction of hatching
- Paper-grain tooth
- Finger-smudged shading
- Eraser-lifted white; hard lines with soft shadows

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: the contrast of hard lines and soft shadow
- **Typography**: no text
- **Color**: monochrome (graphite)
- **Texture & lighting**: paper-grain tooth, smudging, eraser white

## do
- Shade with value and hatching
- Make use of smudging and eraser-lifted white
- **Finish the main elements as "resolved light and shade" through hatching density** (do not leave a draft-like roughness)
- **Cut lightness into the focal element with eraser-lifted white**

## avoid
- Color, ink outlines, digital
- **The roughness of an unfinished sketch**

## Negative
`no color, no ink outline, no digital, no unfinished sketchiness`

## Prompt template
```text
A finished pencil drawing of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}. Graphite value range, directional
hatching carried to a resolved chiaroscuro — not a sketch, paper grain, finger-smudged shading,
eraser-lifted white cutting a bright focal point on the one important element, hard lines with
soft shadows.
```
