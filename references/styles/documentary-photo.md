# Documentary photography（documentary-photo）

- **Medium**: Photography ／ **Lineage**: Social documentary ／ **Era**: 1960s–70s
- **Summary**: Unstaged natural light and film grain record the truth of ordinary life as a quiet report.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `SCENE`＝the place, `LIGHT`＝light, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Unstaged moment, subjects unaware of the camera
- Available natural light, no artificial setup
- 35mm film grain, honest reportage framing
- Ordinary people in real social circumstances
- Muted color or monochrome, modest contrast

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: candid, flat and eye-level, no dramatic posing
- **Typography**: no text
- **Color**: muted film color or monochrome, low saturation
- **Texture & light**: film grain, available daylight, window or street light

## do
- Keep the moment unstaged and report it honestly
- Keep the film grain and natural light

## avoid
- Posed portraits, studio lighting, dramatic color grading, digital polish

## Negative
`no posed studio lighting, no dramatic color grading, no CGI, no illustration`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
A documentary photograph of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {SCENE}, under {LIGHT}.
Unstaged moment, subjects unaware of the camera, available natural light,
35mm film grain, honest reportage framing, ordinary people, muted color
or monochrome, modest contrast.
```
