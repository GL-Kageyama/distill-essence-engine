# Shonen battle manga（shonen-battle）

- **Medium**: Digital ／ **Lineage**: Shonen battle manga ／ **Era**: Contemporary
- **Summary**: Speed lines, impact bursts and dramatic diagonals with high contrast — the kinetic energy of action manga.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Dynamic diagonal composition and dramatic low/high angles
- Speed lines and motion trails
- Impact effects: starbursts, sparks, impact bursts
- Exaggerated motion (stretched poses, sudden scale)
- Bold contrast with a saturated `ACCENT` energy color
- Thick ink lines and solid black fills

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: diagonal action lines, the subject off-center, force lines through the frame
- **Typography**: bold impact sound-effect lettering
- **Color**: high contrast, saturated accents against dark tones
- **Texture & light**: thick lines, black fills, speed-line and dot energy textures

## do
- Throw the composition on a diagonal and exaggerate the motion
- Add speed lines and impact bursts
- Use bold contrast with one energy accent

## avoid
- Static poses, even centered compositions, soft gradient shading, quiet calm scenes

## Negative
`not photorealistic, no static composition, no soft gradient shading, no 3D render`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
A shonen battle-manga illustration of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}, with {ACCENT}.
Dynamic diagonal composition, a dramatic low or high angle, speed lines and impact bursts

thick ink lines and solid black fills, an energy aura.

```
