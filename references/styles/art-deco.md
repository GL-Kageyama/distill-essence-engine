# Art Deco（art-deco）

- **Medium**: Printmaking / decorative ／ **Lineage**: Art Deco ／ **Era**: 1920–30s
- **Summary**: Geometric symmetry and radiance — the opulence of gold leaf and black lacquer — engrave the elegance of the machine age.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Geometric symmetry
- Radiating sunburst
- Streamlined forms
- Gold leaf and black lacquer
- Stepped silhouettes, marble, repeating straight lines

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: symmetry, radiance, stepped layers
- **Typography**: geometric decorative lettering (when needed)
- **Color**: gold leaf, black lacquer, marble white + deep jewel tones
- **Texture & lighting**: gloss and sheen, repeating straight lines, flat opulence without shadow

## do
- Keep the symmetry, radiance, and repetition of straight lines
- Gold leaf and black lacquer contrast
- **Place the subject (one symbol of the content) at the front and keep the ornament to the edges and background**. Use the radiance as the subject's halo

## avoid
- Organic curves, clutter, photorealism
- **Ornament eroding the subject (the ornamental world overwriting the content)**

## Negative
`no organic curves, no clutter, no photorealistic, no ornament overriding the subject`

## Prompt template
```text
An Art Deco poster of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} with {ACCENT}. The one subject at the front, clearly
separate from the ornament; geometric symmetry, a radiating sunburst kept as the subject's
backdrop, streamlined stepped silhouettes, gold leaf and black lacquer over marble white,
repeating straight lines, luxurious flat sheen.
```
