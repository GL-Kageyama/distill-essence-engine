# Gekiga（gekiga）

- **Medium**: Hand-drawn ／ **Lineage**: Gekiga (dramatic comics) ／ **Era**: 1960s–70s
- **Summary**: Realistic black-and-white with dense crosshatching and hard, high-contrast shadows — the adult, psychological, hard-boiled voice of dramatic comics.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Black-and-white, no color
- Realistic human anatomy and faces (no cute cartoon proportions)
- Dense crosshatching and pen linework for shadow
- Hard, high-contrast shadow with pools of ink
- Gritty paper texture
- A quiet menace in the atmosphere

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: cinematic, off-center, deep focus
- **Typography**: plain caption boxes, restrained lettering
- **Color**: monochrome
- **Texture & light**: dense pen hatching, ink pools, gritty grain

## do
- Draw realistically and shade with crosshatch
- Use hard, high-contrast shadow and ink pools
- Keep it monochrome and cinematic

## avoid
- Cartoonish proportions, screentone, bright color, cute faces, soft shading

## Negative
`not photorealistic photography, no color, no screentone, no cute cartoon style, no 3D render`
