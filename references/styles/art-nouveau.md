# Art Nouveau（art-nouveau）

- **Medium**: Printmaking / decorative ／ **Lineage**: Art Nouveau ／ **Era**: 1890–1910
- **Summary**: Whiplash curves and botanical borders wind life around flat surfaces.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Whiplash curves
- Organic botanical borders
- Flat decorative planes
- Stained-glass-like light
- Fine outlines, a vine frame

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: vine frame, organic flow, flat planes
- **Typography**: organic decorative lettering (when needed)
- **Color**: flat planes with jewel tones, gold edges
- **Texture & lighting**: stained-glass-like transmitted light, fine outlines, no shadow

## do
- Make curves and botanical motifs the focus
- Flat planes and fine outlines

## avoid
- Straight grids, photorealism, flat minimalism

## Negative
`no straight grid, no photorealistic, no flat minimal`

## Prompt template
```text
An Art Nouveau illustration of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} with {ACCENT}. Whiplash curves,
organic botanical borders, flat decorative planes, stained-glass-like glow,
fine outlines and a vine frame.
```
