# Flat design（flat-ui）

- **Medium**: Digital ／ **Lineage**: Flat UI (Metro / iOS 7) ／ **Era**: 2010s–present
- **Summary**: Flat single-color surfaces, no gradient, no shadow, simple geometry, crisp edges, wide white space — a clean minimal interface.

## Environment variables
`APP`＝the product, `SCREEN`＝the content of the screen, `ACCENT`＝the single accent color

## Fidelity anchors
- **Flat single-color surfaces**: no gradient, no shadow, no depth, no bevel
- Simple geometry and **crisp edges**; wide white space
- One accent color over neutral grays and white
- Short clear labels; a clean grid

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: a simple grid, one strong focal region, generous white space
- **Typography**: one clean sans, short labels, a clear scale
- **Color**: white / light-gray ground, one accent color, neutrals only beyond it
- **Texture / lighting**: flat, matte, no gradient, no shadow, no texture

## do
- Keep every surface flat — no gradient, no shadow
- Use simple geometry, crisp edges, one accent color
- Leave generous white space

## avoid
- Gradients, shadows, depth, bevels, skeuomorphic texture, more than one accent color, cluttered screens

## Negative
`not gradient, no shadow, no depth, no bevel, no skeuomorphic texture, no more than one accent color`

## Prompt template
```text
A flat-design screen of {APP}. {SCREEN} arranged on a clean grid — flat single-color
surfaces, crisp edges, simple geometry, no gradient, no shadow, generous white space.
One accent color {ACCENT} over white and light-gray neutrals. Short clear labels,
the whole screen matte and flat.
```
