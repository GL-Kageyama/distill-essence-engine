# Sources of the online research

> External sources referenced to distill the vocabulary of the pre-expanded cards (18 styles · 7 formats). The cards are not "copies of raw prompts" but the distillation of this vocabulary into the engine's own form (medium × lineage × era / vocabulary / negative pairs).

## OSS (downloaded to incorporate structure and templates)

- [VigoZhao/AI-Visual-Prompt-Cookbook](https://github.com/VigoZhao/AI-Visual-Prompt-Cookbook)（MIT）— style.json v2.1 schema (environment variables · fidelity anchors · visual decomposition · do/avoid · prompt_template · negative · examples). The foundation of `references/card-schema.md`.
- [freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2](https://github.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2)（MIT）— the generation workflow (category matching · 6 blocks · multi-option presentation) and the Prompt-as-Code idea. Reflected in step 2 of SKILL.md.

## Style vocabulary

- [MidJourney-Styles-and-Keywords-Reference（LOVECHEN）](https://github.com/LOVECHEN/MidJourney-Styles-and-Keywords-Reference) — a list of styles and keywords
- [supagruen/stablediffusion-cheatsheet](https://github.com/supagruen/stablediffusion-cheatsheet) — a cheatsheet of manually verified styles (833+)
- [Style Prompt Guide（naetharu.com）](https://naetharu.com/posts/aiArt/style-prompts/) — a comparison of oil painting, gouache, watercolor, impressionism
- [Artistic Styles SREF Codes（promptgenius.net）](https://www.promptgenius.net/prompts/midjourney/sref/artistic) — a collection of style codes (vintage engraving / neon nostalgia etc.)

## Composition · formats

- [GPT-Image2-Skill craft.md（wuyoscar）](https://github.com/wuyoscar/GPT-Image2-Skill/blob/main/skills/gpt-image/references/craft.md) — the composition grammar of posters, storyboards, and icons (shot types · camera language · treatment of text)
- [AI Style Prompt Pack（naviya.chat）](https://www.naviya.chat/zh-CN/blog/ai-style-prompt-pack-for-brand-visuals) — reusable style recipes (copperplate engraving · retro pixel etc.)
- [GPT Image 2 Prompt Guide（image2.im）](https://image2.im/tw/blog/gpt-image-2-prompt-guide) — the principles of asset-name-first, single style, single subject

## Prompt structure

- [Midjourney Prompt Engineering Guide（10b.ai）](https://10b.ai/blog/midjourney-prompt-engineering) — subject-first, adjust one block at a time
- [Stable Diffusion Prompt: A Definitive Guide（neura.market）](https://www.neura.market/directories/stable-diffusion/prompts/stable-diffusion-prompt-a-definitive-guide) — Subject + Style + Quality + Lighting + Composition + Details
