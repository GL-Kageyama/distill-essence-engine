The phone is laid face-down with one small deliberate press of the fingers. It buzzes ONCE — a short dull weighted rattle against wood, not a slide — and she does not turn it over; her eyes move and her body does not. Later she lifts it with both hands. The figure inside the screen moves loosely and warmly, far more freely than the girl ever does. Ordinary weight and inertia everywhere. The phone never moves by itself and never glitches, flickers or distorts; its screen changes only by ordinary UI transitions. The light from beneath the face-down phone appears steadily, like an ordinary screen simply being on, never pulsing or strobing. ## 生成したら最初に見る5点

1. **アプリ欄が「会話」「部活」になっているか** — アプリ名やアイコンが出たらこの話の開示は消える。**最優先**
2. **回想2カットにスマホが写っていないか** — 写っていたら核が無効
3. **伏せたスマホが下から光っているか** — 裏返す前に、机に光の縁が出ること
4. **ニジが透けていないか** — 第4話も完全に不透明（薄くなるのは第8話以降）
5. **「どうせ」を先に言っているか** — 0:26。真白より先でなければ、読まれた感じが出ない
