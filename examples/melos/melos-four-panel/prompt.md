マンガ線画：白黒インク線、スクリーントーン、ベタ。吹き出しは「三日、いや三日」「遅れれば、友を殺す」「まだ陽は沈まぬ」。

A four-panel manga of Run, Melos!, the tyrant's cruel three-day promise: 4 panels stacked in a fixed vertical column, a constant camera angle across all panels, kishotenketsu rhythm — panel 1 sets the scene (Melos dragged before the pale king), panel 2 develops it (three days' grace granted with the friend as hostage), panel 3 turns it (Melos bolts from the city, the sun already low), panel 4 delivers the punchline (the frantic sprint over the mountain, the shout "the sun has not set yet!"), short speech bubbles with "three days", "come late and he dies", "the sun has not set yet!", minimal background, static framing, clean and legible.
