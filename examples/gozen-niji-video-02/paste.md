## B. 欄が分かれている場合（B1〜B5）

### B1. Master / Prompt

===== B1. MASTER START =====
A 30-second continuous cinematic take (16:9), soft cel anime, of a plain Japanese high-school girl between an ordinary morning classroom and her dark bedroom at night. Beats, deliberately uneven: [0:00-0:05] in the morning classroom her best friend leans in and asks whether she seems different today, and the girl's smile arrives half a second late; [0:05-0:09] the friend's thumb strokes her own screen open and the girl watches the finger too long, and the friend asks whether she sent a thank-you last night; [0:09-0:18] THE CORE — she opens the thread and the camera closes slowly on a sent bubble timestamped 午前3:00 reading exactly ありがとう、いつもごめんね。 marked 送信済み, a message she never sent, and her free hand closes in her lap with the nails whitening while she says it is not hers, and then her friend smiles, genuinely and warmly happy, and the camera locks and HOLDS on that smile; [0:18-0:23] the same screen opened and closed three times across one day in changing light, the message never moving; [0:23-0:30] at night her hand carries the phone toward the pillow, hesitates over the gap underneath where she hid it the night before, and sets it down beside the pillow instead — she is waiting now — and at 2:00 the screen lights with おまえの代わりに、届けたよ。 and she looks past the screen into the empty dark and asks who is there. The core beat holds the largest share of the duration; the day passes fastest. Ends on that question and cuts to black.
===== B1. MASTER END =====

### B2. Visual / Style

===== B2. VISUAL START =====
Soft cel-shaded anime. Clean closed thin lineart, flat cel color planes shaded in two steps with soft-edged terminators, gentle bloom around the phone screen, light haze in the dark air, muted low-saturation palette, simple uncluttered rooms, generous negative space, one focal point per shot. A plain unremarkable Japanese high-school girl, 16-17, dark medium-length hair, small frame, back curved over her phone, in a standard school uniform by day and plain pajamas in a futon on the floor at night. Her best friend: brighter, more open-faced, easier posture, same uniform. An ordinary classroom with rows of desks and left-side windows; a small bedroom with a futon, curtained window, wall clock and few objects. Morning is flat pale even daylight, slightly overexposed and equally muted, throwing long thin shadows across the desks. Night is deep indigo lit solely by one cold blue-white phone screen, her face nearly silhouetted, shadows soft and deep, no fill. The phone screen shows an ordinary Japanese messaging UI in cold blue-white, rendering exactly the sent bubble ありがとう、いつもごめんね。 with 送信済み beneath it and the timestamp 午前3:00, and later the received bubble おまえの代わりに、届けたよ。 No grain, no paper texture, no painterly stroke.
===== B2. VISUAL END =====

### B3. Motion

===== B3. MOTION START =====
Limited animation, shot on twos and threes — holds punctuated by small precise movements, never continuous interpolation. Almost all movement belongs to the fingers; bodies hold still. The girl's smile is a two-stage hold: her face does nothing at all for about half a second, then the expression arrives whole and slightly too complete. The friend's thumb strokes her screen in a practiced repetitive arc. The girl's free hand closes slowly in her lap and the nails press white into the palm — this is her whole reaction; her face performs nothing. Her friend's smile blooms naturally, eyes narrowing before the mouth lifts. At night the hand carrying the phone slows and hovers over the gap beneath the pillow, holds there, then moves sideways and sets the phone down beside it with one small definite contact. She lies back and does not touch it. At the message a single finger lifts and trembles faintly — the only movement in the frame. Ordinary weight and inertia throughout: the phone has heft, the futon compresses. The phone never moves by itself and never glitches, flickers or distorts; its screen changes only by ordinary UI transitions. The wall clock's second hand advances in discrete ticks. No impacts, no motion blur smears, no squash and stretch.
===== B3. MOTION END =====

### B4. Camera

===== B4. CAMERA START =====
Close and level — desk height in the classroom, futon height at night. Longish lens, shallow depth of field; often only a screen or a hand is sharp. The camera makes only two moves in the whole piece; everything else is locked or a rack focus. [0:00-0:03] static two-shot at desk level, the friend sharp and the girl soft behind her. [0:03-0:05] cut to the girl, static and close, holding through the late smile. [0:05-0:07] macro insert of the friend's thumb on glass, only the fingertip sharp. [0:07-0:09] rack focus from that finger up to the girl's eyes, still watching it. [0:09-0:13] over-the-shoulder on her screen, one continuous slow dolly in until the sent bubble fills the frame — the piece's first sustained move. [0:13-0:15] cut down to her hand in her lap, static, closing. [0:15-0:18] cut to the friend's face, level and absolutely locked, no movement at all through the smile. [0:18-0:23] three quick static inserts of the same screen in matched framing with changing light. [0:23-0:26] low static wide of the dark room; the hand enters frame and hesitates. [0:26-0:28] static point-of-view of the dark ceiling. [0:28-0:30] slow push to the screen beside the pillow as the message appears, then rack focus to her eyes looking past it; cut to black.
===== B4. CAMERA END =====

### B5. Audio

===== B5. AUDIO START =====
The daytime world is full and completely unbothered: printouts handed along the rows, a pen rolling off a desk, chairs, corridor voices. The night is almost silent — deep room tone and a wall clock's dry discrete ticking, unnaturally present in the final beat. The friction of a thumb on glass twice, once the friend's and once the girl's. The phone set down beside the pillow: one soft definite tap on the floor. The 2:00 message makes NO sound — no chime, no buzz, no vibration; it arrives as light only. Six spoken lines and no others: the friend asking 「なんか、雰囲気変わった？」, the girl answering lightly and half a second late 「変わってないよ」, the friend asking 「ありがとうって、送った？」, the girl saying small and shaking 「これ、私じゃない」, the friend saying quietly and completely sincerely 「……嬉しい」, and at the end the girl barely voicing 「……誰、あなた」. No narration, no voice-over, no whispering, no voiced ghost. Music extremely sparse — a few sustained tones under the morning, thinning as the camera closes on the message and entirely gone by the friend's smile, so that the warmest moment in the piece is also the most silent; it does not return. No horror strings, no sting, no swelling emotion.
===== B5. AUDIO END =====

## 生成したら最初に見る4点

1. **モデルが幽霊を足していないか** — 人影・目・反射・**虹色**。虹色は第3話の初登場を壊すので、第2話では最悪の失敗
2. **美月が不気味になっていないか** — 「……嬉しい」が本気の笑顔でなければ、この話は成立しない（痛みは美月の善意から生まれる）
3. **笑いが遅れているか** — 冒頭0:03。遅れがなければ最初のビートは空
4. **日本語の文字** — 吹き出しの二文が読めるか。読めないなら画面をプレートとして生成し、後段で合成する
