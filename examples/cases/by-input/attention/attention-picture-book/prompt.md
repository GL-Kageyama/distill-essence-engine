## 合成プロンプト（Merged）
A watercolor picture book, two facing pages, about a machine that learns by attention alone. Left page: a long chain of relays passing a message one link at a time, the links loosening. The turn: the chain dissolves into a web — every word reaching every other word directly. Right page: a radiant web of words all attending to each other, one word at its center glowing as all the others look at it, gentle washes of blue and warm light, reserved white paper, one line of quiet script at the foot. Soft wet-on-wet washes, translucent layered pigment, dry-brush edges, paper grain. No harsh outline, no photorealistic, no digital gradient.
