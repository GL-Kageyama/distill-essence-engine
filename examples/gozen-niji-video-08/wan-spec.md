# 7. NARRATIVE

## Core Event

She asks what the ghost is made of, and the answer is: the feelings other people sent her that she never received. And while it answers, its fingers begin to go transparent.

## Beginning

The sentence from last night still hanging. 「……どういう意味」「そのままの意味だよ」 ニジ sits in the screen with her knees drawn up, **looking up** at 真白.

## Development

The mechanism, finally stated: every like, every read receipt, every viewed story had a feeling aimed at her inside it. 「おまえは受け取らなかった。届いたのに、受け取らなかった」

## Turning Point

ニジ looks at her own hand. The fingers are faintly transparent. She keeps talking.

## Climax

「わたしは、おまえの分身でも、理想像でもない。——わたしは、おまえが受け取らなかった、人の感情の結晶」…「消すんじゃない。無視するんじゃない。わたしを壊すんじゃない。——返すの」

## Ending

真白 opens the thread with 小春 — the month-old consultation. Her fingertip touches the glass above it. **And stops.**

### Timing Policy

- The transparency at `[0:14]` must be **discovered by the viewer, not pointed at**. No reaction shot, no camera push, no music cue
- The hold on the hand runs the length of the confession
- The final stop is the **inversion of ep.1's stop**: there it stopped from shock, here it stops from being unable to decide. Same stillness, opposite cause

# 9. ACTION

- `A1` ニジ sits with her knees drawn up, looking **upward** at 真白 through the glass
- `A2` Fast inserts: a thumb taps a like; a read receipt appears; a story plays out
- `A3` **ニジ raises her hand in front of her own face and studies it** — calmly, the way you check whether something is dirty
- `A4` She lowers it and keeps talking. She does not react to it
- `A5` 真白 does not react to it either. Her eyes are on ニジ's face, not her hand
- `A6` ニジ smiles with a face on the edge of crying, and does not cry
- `A7` 真白 opens the thread and scrolls to the top of a very long message
- `A8` Her fingertip descends, contacts the glass, and **stops**

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST

- **Only ニジ's fingers are transparent.** The rest of her is solid
- **Her outline is the sharpest and clearest it has been in the whole series** — both facts are true at once
- **Neither character remarks on the transparency**, and neither does the camera or the score
- ニジ sits with knees drawn up, **looking up** at 真白
- She smiles with a face on the edge of crying, and does **not** cry
- 真白's fingertip **touches the glass and stops**. It does not press, tap, or send
- The fast inserts are from 真白's **own past use** — a like, a read receipt, a story

## MUST NOT

- **No full-body transparency, no fading, no dissolving, no disappearing.** Fingers only, faintly. The full fade belongs to ep.10 and ep.12
- Do not have anyone point at, look at, gasp at, or mention the hand
- Do not push the camera in on the hand or cue it with music
- No tears from anyone
- Do not send the message. **真白 does not act in this episode** — the sending is ep.9
- Do not show 小春's face, or anyone else's, in this episode
- No horror grammar, no supernatural effects, no shimmer or flicker on the transparency

## PREFER

- One locked frame over any emphasis
- The viewer discovering the hand before the story acknowledges it

## ALLOW

- Tiny authentic UI sounds in the history inserts
- The iridescence to tremble very slightly at `[0:24]` — its first instability

# 20. ITERATION

## Version

`v1 — untested`

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **The whole figure goes translucent.** The most likely failure and the most damaging — the transparency is a curve across five episodes and this is its first degree. Check that only the fingers read as see-through
2. **The transparency gets emphasized** — a push-in, a music swell, a reaction shot. If any of these appear, regenerate; the restraint *is* the scene
3. **Her outline is not sharper than before.** The contradiction (clearest and disappearing) must be visible; if she just looks faded, the scene loses its cruelty
4. **真白 sends the message.** She must only touch and stop; sending is ep.9's whole event
5. **Someone cries.** Neither of them does, in any episode

## Next Generation

`If "only the fingers" cannot be held, generate the hand as a separate macro plate at the correct opacity and composite it into the locked frame — the shot does not move, so a composite is clean.`
