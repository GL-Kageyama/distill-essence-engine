# 7. NARRATIVE

## Core Event

At the top of a list of everyone she has ever given time to is a boy she has never spoken to — and the number is four hours and fifty-two minutes.

## Beginning

2:00. A screen behind the screen-time settings that should not exist: thirty-two names, each with a duration.

## Development

Her finger scrolls. Names she knows, names she had forgotten, names that never answered. Toward the top the numbers grow.

## Turning Point

**氷室湊……4時間52分.** The longest. A boy whose back she has been watching for a year.

## Climax

ニジ teases her about it, delightedly, and 真白 is embarrassed — the first ordinary teenage feeling in six episodes. 「この人、最後に返すと、いいよ」…「いちばん返したら、何かが変わるから」

## Ending

Next day. He passes her in the corridor at speed, carrying a bundle of committee papers, **and does not see her**. She watches his back until it turns the corner and is gone.

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

## Temporal Units

`Five beats. The tonal outlier of the series: the middle of this episode is warm and funny, and the ending is not sad so much as quiet.`

## Temporal Sequence

| Time | Beat | Content | Density |
|---|---|---|---|

### Timing Policy

- The scroll in `[0:00–0:07]` is slow and continuous, not flicked
- The reveal of the top name holds for a full beat **before** anyone speaks
- The comedy in `[0:14–0:20]` is genuine and light. Do not undercut it
- **`[0:27–0:30]` is the episode's translation**: the "outline of deposited time" on his back is made of **duration and silence**, not of any visual effect

### Temporal Density

`Flowing → held → warm and busy → slowing → one long unnatural hold on a receding back.`

# 9. ACTION

- `A1` Her fingertip rests on the list; it flows under it, slowly and continuously
- `A2` Two brief memories surface and sink — a desk, a club room — without narration
- `A3` She reverses the scroll and moves **upward**
- `A4` Her finger stops on the top name and stays there, long
- `A5` **ニジ grins** — wide, delighted, teasing. She leans toward the glass
- `A6` ニジ places her own finger on the number from the inside of the screen
- `A7` 真白 half-covers her face with one hand — embarrassment, not distress
- `A8` In the corridor, 湊 walks fast, arms full, eyes ahead. **He does not turn his head**
- `A9` 真白 turns her whole body to follow him with her eyes, and does not move otherwise

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST

- The list renders in the source's layout, including **氷室湊……4時間52分** at the top and **美月…………3時間14分 ／ お母さん……1時間02分 ／ 小春…………0時間47分** below
- The scroll moves **upward** toward larger numbers
- ニジ **grins and teases**, and 真白 is **embarrassed, not frightened**
- 湊 **does not look at 真白** as he passes
- The final shot **holds on his receding back longer than is natural**, and the corridor's sound drains away
- ニジ is **fully opaque**

## MUST NOT

- **No glow, aura, outline, silhouette effect, or any visual overlay on 湊's back.** The "outline of deposited time" is made of **held duration and drained sound** — rendering it as an effect destroys the translation and breaks the world's rule that the supernatural is never staged
- **ニジ must not be translucent or fading.** Transparency begins in ep.8
- **ニジ must not say 「わたし」** — her first use is the climax of the *next* episode, and spending it here destroys it
- Do not make 湊 romantic-lit, slow-motion, wind-blown, or handsome in a shoujo register. He is an ordinary busy third-year
- Do not have him notice her, glance back, or half-turn
- Do not make the list beat frightening. It is intimate
- No tears, no distress, no horror grammar

## PREFER

- Duration and silence over any effect
- Genuine lightness in the middle — let it be funny

## ALLOW

- More secondary motion than any other episode
- A warmer, more active iridescence during the teasing
- A camera move that fails (the late whip-pan) — this is intentional

# 20. ITERATION

## Version

`v1 — untested`

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **湊's back glows.** The likeliest failure — models will visualize "the outline of her deposited time" literally. The Negative front-loads every word for it. If it appears, remove the metaphor from the Master slot entirely and describe only the held shot and the silence
2. **The middle is not funny.** If the tone stays grim, the series loses its only relief and ep.9–12's cost stops registering. Regenerate with "teasing, delighted, embarrassed" in the Master slot
3. **湊 is shot romantically** — slow motion, wind, golden light. He is ordinary and in a hurry
4. **The scroll goes downward** or the top name is not the largest number
5. **ニジ says わたし one episode early** — check the audio

## Next Generation

`If the whip-pan reads as an error rather than as intention, replace it with a simple late static cut — the meaning (she was too slow) survives either way.`
