A Japanese woodblock print (mokuhanga) of one moment from a Greek legend. A young shepherd, gaunt and half-naked, his sandals broken, has collapsed on a rocky roadside at sunset. He crouches low, drinking from a small clear spring that wells from a crack in a gray boulder — the water bright against his mud-streaked body. The low sun sets the leaves and branches alight, crimson. Beyond, the road climbs a mountain pass toward a distant walled city, its single white tower tiny in the red glow. Off-center composition: the kneeling man and the spring sit low-left, the empty road and the burning sky recede to the upper right toward the far city. Flat layered planes, visible wood grain, bold black sumi outlines, black and crimson, no text.
