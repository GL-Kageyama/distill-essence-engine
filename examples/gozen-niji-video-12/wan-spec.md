# 7. NARRATIVE

## Core Event

She types, with her own finger, the sentence that appeared on her screen by itself in the first episode — and the ghost, having been paid back in full, takes her color back and goes.

## Beginning

2 A.M. The ledger's one remaining record, addressed to herself. ニジ almost invisible, **her hair gone white**. 「……最後の宛先、分かったよ」

## Development

「これは、私が、自分に、預けた、時間だ」…「『どうせ、誰も、私を、見てない』って、——自分を粗末にした時間を」 **The refrain finally gets an addressee, and the addressee is her.** 「ニジ、あなたは、私の感情も、含んでたんだね」「うん。——おまえが自分に向けた言葉も、——ちゃんと集まってたよ」

## Turning Point

「……返すよ」 **She types it herself.**

## Climax

「……返せた？」「うん。——返せた」 ニジ smiles — a smile 真白 has never seen and has somehow seen before. 「返してくれて、ありがとう」「——おまえがくれた、時間。——ちゃんと、生きて、きたよ」 **The white light slowly takes back its color.**

## Ending

「——行ってらっしゃい」 The iridescence dissolves into light. Morning: **今日、あなたが誰かに預けた時間はありません** ／ **また明日**. She closes the phone. The futon has no heat left in it. She opens the curtain. 「……うん。また、明日」

# 11. MOTION

- Subject Motion: `Limited animation, twos and threes. The typing is the most deliberate movement in the whole series — slow, unhurried, character by character, a thumb that has stopped ten times finally completing something`
- Object Motion: `**The white→iridescent transition is a slow shift of color within a held shape** — not a flicker, not a wave, not a pulse. **The dissolve is slow and even**, inward into the light of the screen, and it stays inside the screen`
- Environmental Motion: `A curtain opening. Nothing else`
- Physical Characteristics: `**ニジ dissolves; she does not shatter, scatter, burst, or fly away.** She has no wings, no particles, no ascent. She goes into the light of a phone screen at 2 A.M.`

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST

- **The sentence is typed by 真白, character by character, in a macro framed identically to ep.1's**
- The typed text reads exactly **おまえが私にくれた時間、私が生きてるよ。** and the addressee is **her own name**
- ニジ's sequence is **white light → slowly regaining the iridescence → dissolving into light**, in that order
- **She dissolves inside the screen.** Not in the room, not upward, not away
- ニジ's last smile is **relieved and kind** — the expression 真白 hoped to be able to show herself
- 「行ってらっしゃい」 is **not a goodbye**; it is what someone at home says to someone leaving
- 真白's voice **shakes on 「……うん」 and she does not stop it**
- The morning notification reads **今日、あなたが誰かに預けた時間はありません** with **また明日** beneath it, in smaller type
- **When the phone closes, the room's light source changes from the screen to the window**
- The futon is **empty and flat**, with no heat left in it
- The last line is **「……うん。また、明日」** and nothing follows it

## MUST NOT

- **No tears. From anyone.** Twelve episodes without a single one. A shaking voice is the whole allowance
- **No moral, no summary, no explanation at the end.** Nothing states what the story meant
- **Do not shoot the ending as uplift.** No running, no arms outstretched, no rooftop, no city panorama, no crane-up, no golden hour, no sunrise over anything, no lens flare, no uplifting music, no choir, no smile held for the camera, no wind in the hair
- **The camera does not go outside with her.** It stays inside the doorway
- ニジ does not shatter, scatter, burst into particles, sprout wings, ascend, or fly. **She dissolves, slowly, into a phone screen**
- Do not make the dissolve sound. No chime, no whoosh, no sparkle
- Do not skip the white-hair stage, and do not skip the return of color
- Do not let the typing be fast or casual. It is the most deliberate act in the series

## PREFER

- The morning's light being ordinary weather, and dazzling only because her eyes are unused to it
- Five quiet holds for the ending rather than four shots and a flourish
- The lighting change carrying the arc, with no line of dialogue to help it

## ALLOW

- The wall clock reading somewhere past 2:00 during the dissolve — the source says 「午前二時の、あと」. **`3:21` is available and is the end time of the ep.1 record**, i.e. the hour is fully returned at the moment it was originally taken. This is an inference, not the source's statement — use it or don't
- One very slight musical rise as the color returns — the only rise in the series

# 20. ITERATION

## Version

`v1 — untested`

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **The ending becomes uplift.** The strongest attractor in the entire twelve-episode set: a girl walking out into morning light *demands* golden hour, a crane-up, and a swell. Every one of those is forbidden, and if any appears the series' restraint — the thing the whole thing is built on — is spent in its last two seconds
2. **ニジ scatters into particles or ascends.** She dissolves, slowly, evenly, **into the screen**. Any upward or outward motion turns a return into an apotheosis
3. **The white→iridescent stage is skipped**, and she simply fades. Then the ending reads as death rather than return, and the color 真白 named in ep.3 never comes back
4. **The typing is not framed like ep.1.** The rhyme is the core of the episode: same shot, same sentence, and the only difference is who makes it. If the framing is new, twelve episodes of setup collect nothing
5. **Someone cries**, or a line explains what it all meant. Either one undoes the series

## Next Generation

`If the white→iridescent shift cannot be held without flicker, generate the two states as separate plates in the same locked frame and cross-dissolve between them slowly — the shot does not move, so the blend is clean.`
