## 合成プロンプト（Merged）
Woodblock-print storyboard, 16:9, six panels left to right, each cut the consequence of the previous beat. Panel 1 WIDE: a bare-chested shepherd stands before a tyrant king, eyes locked. Panel 2 OTS: he runs from the city gate — the consequence of that vow, a crimson mantle streaming. Panel 3 CU: the bridge snapped mid-stream, water roaring, his shadow on the bank — the deadline closing. Panel 4 CU: he drinks at a spring in a rock cleft, broken sandal beside him, strength returning. Panel 5 LOW ANGLE: he sprints down a night street, torches blurring. Panel 6 WIDE: two men embrace at the foot of an empty cross — the promise kept. The same character throughout — half-naked, sandals, crimson mantle; the same black-and-crimson woodblock palette; hand-carved ink lines, chisel marks, flat mineral planes. Not photorealistic, no digital gradient, no 3D render.
