## 内容（Content）

**②選択＝この一枚が運ぶのは「暗がりに浮く青白い火」の一点だけ。** サムネイルの型は**一点を強く、まわりを削る**。ゆえに**人を描かない**——小さく描いた二人は小さい画面で読めず、火を弱める。

**③翻訳＝二つ。** 火、火を映す二本のレール。

**捨てたもの。** 腕木・踏切の板・砂利・水たまり・人影。すべて削る。

## フォーマット（Format）

16:9。**火は中央やや上、画面の幅の五分の一ほど。** レール二本が手前から火の下へ収束する。

- **まわりは黒。** 光の届く範囲だけを描く。
- **文字なし。**
- **人物なし。**

## 様式（Style）

灯ひとつ。**固いものはセル、空気は写実。**

**衝突と解決**：

1. **「一点を強く」と「光源は小さい」** → 火は**小さく、しかし高コントラスト**。芯を明るく、縁の赤を細く保つ。**大きくするのでなく、まわりの黒を広く取る。**
2. **「レール」と「まわりを削る」** → レールは**光を受けた二本の線だけ**。枕木を描かない。
3. **「小さな画面」と「霧」** → 霧は**火のまわりの狭い範囲だけ**。画面全体を曇らせない。
4. **「人物なし」** → 誰も描かない。**手も袖も入らない。**

## 合成プロンプト（Merged）

A 16:9 thumbnail for 受け火 — one strong focal point, everything else carved away, striking at small size. One point only. The soul-fire: a flame no larger than a closed fist in all, floating low above the rails, held by no one, softly and palely blue-white — a low-saturation body, palest at the centre, cooling to a faint blue at the edge, with only a little red at the rim: one thin, faint, rust-red edge, never a crimson outline, never spreading into an orange flame. Its round body takes up the lower part of the flame, about as wide as it is tall; from its top a short, thin thread rises, no taller than the body itself, bending only slightly. The outline is one closed contour, with no tongue licking sideways. The mist around it holds the light softly, as an even pool that fades into the dark. No smoke, no steam, no sparks, no embers. It is the only light in the frame, it casts one shadow for each body it lights, and it gives no heat. No moonlight, no starlight, no sky light, no ambient fill, no bounce light; whatever the fire does not reach falls into unbroken black. The fire sits a little above the centre of the frame and is about a fifth of its width. Two rails run in from the bottom corners and meet under the fire, catching the light along their heads as two thin bright lines and nothing else; no sleepers, no gravel, no boards, no barrier arm and no person are drawn. Everything outside the light is unbroken black that takes up most of the frame. Solid things are painted: every surface carries its texture and its modelling, and the values step into clear bands rather than a gradient laid across a surface. Only the air is soft, and only close around the fire — a narrow pool of thin mist and fine particles catching the light; the rest of the frame is clear black. The palette is sunken indigo, rust red, pale white-blue and black. No readable text, no signage, no numbers, no logos, no Japanese or English lettering anywhere in the frame. There is no sky — no stars, no moon, no sun, no clouds, no horizon glow. No rain falls and no drop is falling anywhere in the frame. No bloom, no lens flare, no ring around the fire, no second light source. Not photorealistic, no 3D render, no metallic sheen. Every figure is finished as the place behind it is painted, never as a flat diagram: the skin holds a warm pale value with blood under it, a soft shadow under the brow and along the side of the jaw, and a little more warmth in the cheeks and on the lips — a warmth that stays inside the skin as a faint peach, carried as shading at every size of the face, so the full figure and the enlarged head show one and the same faint tone; the lips are drawn, closed, one step deeper than the skin; each eye carries a dark iris with a small light in it and a soft ring of shadow under the lid; the hair is drawn in separated strands that fall with weight, with a soft sheen along the crown where the light lays; the cloth carries its weave, with soft folds where it creases and a worn edge where it wears. The clothing and the hair keep a value above the night: the cloth holds one clear step of value above the black around the figure, the hair holds a value above the black too, its darkest tone still reading as a colour in shadow and its sheen as a pale edge where the fire catches it, so a fold or a lock even in shadow reads as cloth or hair and never as black, and the hair and the cloth always differ by at least one step of value.
