# 7. NARRATIVE

## Core Event

She opens every screen she has, expecting to find the hours she wasted — and finds that every one of them has someone's name on it.

## Beginning

The eve of the school festival. A whole day spent moving: decorations, a long banner, the gym stage, a whiteboard written and erased and written again. **She barely touched her phone all day.**

## Development

After school, alone in the emptied classroom at dusk, she sits at her own desk and opens everything — settings, screen time, per-app, the addressee list, the ledger.

## Turning Point

Every record has a name on it. **逃げた時間は、ひとつもなかった.** And her thumb begins to stroke the screen — the exact motion from ep.1 — but now it is stroking names.

## Climax

「……ひとつも、逃げてないね」「うん。——おまえの時間は、——ひとつも無駄じゃ、なかったよ」

## Ending

「……ニジ、教えて」「あなたは、——何なの」 ニジ blinks — and then **looks 真白 in the eye for the first time**. 「わたしは」…「——わたしは、おまえが、誰かに、預けた、時間が、——集まった、姿だよ」

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

## Temporal Units

`Five beats. The episode is a rhyme with ep.1 and must be cut as one: ep.1 opened on a thumb stroking a feed; this one places the same thumb on names, and gives the naming of the ghost the last six seconds.`

## Temporal Sequence

| Time | Beat | Content | Density |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[0:00–0:06]` | 前夜の一日 | The day compressed and **full**: classroom decorations going up, someone hanging a long banner far down the corridor, the gym stage being checked, a whiteboard written / erased / written again. Hands everywhere, work everywhere. **A phone appears in no more than one fragment, briefly** | RAPID — a full day, inverted from ep.4 |
| `[0:06–0:10]` | 空いた教室 | After school. Origami scraps on the desk, the leftovers of tomorrow's decorations. Everyone gone. Someone carrying a sign, heard far off, fading out. The sun tipping past the window. **One classroom light goes off.** She sits at her own desk and opens the phone | SPARSE — the quietest air in the series |
| `[0:10–0:19]` | **核**：全部に名前が付いている | Settings, screen time, per-app, the addressee list, the ledger — opened one after another, quickly, then left open. Every record carries a name. Then **the thumb begins to stroke** — the identical arc from ep.1 — across 美月, お母さん, 小春, and it **stops longest** on one name. 「私の時間、——全部、宛先付いてるね」「うん。付いてる」「……ひとつも、逃げてないね」「うん。——おまえの時間は、——ひとつも無駄じゃ、なかったよ」 | **DENSE → the stroke → HOLD** |
| `[0:19–0:24]` | 暗い教室 | The classroom fully dark now, tomorrow's preparations still spread across the desks. The screen's light on her face is the only light in the room. 「……ニジ、教えて」「なに？」「あなたは、——何なの」 | Slow |
| `[0:24–0:30]` | **わたしは** | ニジ blinks, caught off guard. The iridescence slowly gathers into outline — and **for the first time she raises her eyes and looks directly at 真白**, with 真白's own face. 「わたしは」— a pause — 「——わたしは、おまえが、誰かに、預けた、時間が、——集まった、姿だよ」 Cut to black on the last word | Hook — the naming |

### Timing Policy

- The day beat must feel **full and good**, not frantic. This is a life being lived
- The stroke at `[0:15–0:18]` is the series' central rhyme. It must be **the same speed, the same arc, the same framing** as ep.1's opening stroke. A viewer who has seen ep.1 should recognize it before they understand it
- The final line is delivered **haltingly**, in the source's broken rhythm — see §14
- Cut to black on 「姿だよ」. Nothing after it

### Temporal Density

`Full → empty → dense then slow → still → the halting line. The episode's shape is a day emptying out into one sentence.`

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST

- **The thumb stroke at `[0:14–0:18]` matches ep.1's opening stroke exactly** — same speed, same arc, same macro framing — but over names
- Every visible record carries a **person's name**. No anonymous entries, no app icons in the ledger
- **ニジ says 「わたし」** — her first use of the pronoun in the series
- **ニジ looks 真白 directly in the eye** — her first direct look
- The final line is delivered **haltingly**, assembling itself
- ニジ is **fully opaque**
- Cut to black on 「姿だよ」

## MUST NOT

- **Do not reveal ep.8's disclosure.** In this episode ニジ says only that she is *deposited time, gathered into a shape*. She must **not** say or imply that she is made of **feelings 真白 failed to receive** — that is the next episode's climax and the story's deepest reveal
- **ニジ must not be translucent or fading.** Transparency begins in ep.8, one episode from now. She is at full opacity here — this is the last episode in which that is true
- Do not let the halting delivery become crying, trembling, or solemn portent
- Do not show 真白 using her phone during the day beat more than once, briefly
- Do not put a clock in this episode
- 湊 does not appear, and the name the thumb rests on longest is **not identified on screen**
- No horror grammar; no supernatural effects

## PREFER

- The day to feel good — full, ordinary, worth having
- The rhyme to be recognized before it is understood
- Silence under the final line

## ALLOW

- The name under the resting thumb to be illegible or out of focus
- The iridescence to be dimmer and slower than in ep.6

# 20. ITERATION

## Version

`v1 — untested`

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **The stroke does not match ep.1.** The series' central rhyme lives or dies here. Generate this beat with ep.1's own Motion and Camera language pasted in verbatim if necessary, and compare the two frames side by side before accepting
2. **ニジ renders translucent one episode early.** She is opaque here for the last time; if she fades now, ep.8–12's cost curve has no starting point
3. **The final line is played as grief** — trembling, tearful, portentous. It is a first sentence being assembled, not an emotional climax
4. **The day beat reads as stressful** rather than full and good. If so, slow the cuts and add more hands, more collaboration
5. **The ledger shows app icons** instead of names

## Next Generation

`If the ep.1 rhyme cannot be matched by prompt alone, generate the macro thumb shot as a separate pass using ep.1's Motion/Camera slots unchanged, and cut it into this episode — the shots are meant to be identical, so reusing the language is correct rather than a shortcut.`
