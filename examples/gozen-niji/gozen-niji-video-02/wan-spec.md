# 7. NARRATIVE

## Core Event

The sentence 真白 could never send arrives in her best friend's hands — sent from her own account, at 3 A.M., while she slept. And 美月 is genuinely, uncomplicatedly happy about it.

## Beginning

Morning classroom. 美月 says 真白 seems different today. 真白 laughs it off — **a beat too late**.

## Development

美月 opens her messaging app; her thumb strokes the screen in exactly the gesture 真白 knows in her own hand. She asks whether 真白 sent a thank-you last night.

## Turning Point

真白 opens the thread. 3:00 A.M. — a sent message she did not send: ありがとう、いつもごめんね。 Her hand closes in her lap, nails into her palm. "これ、私じゃない."

## Climax

美月 says 「……嬉しい」 — and means it. The words 真白 spent months typing and deleting have landed, and they worked, and she did not deliver them.

## Ending

That night 真白 puts the phone **beside** the pillow. Yesterday she hid it underneath. Tonight she is waiting. At 2:00 the screen lights: おまえの代わりに、届けたよ。 She looks past the screen — nothing there. 「……誰、あなた」

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

## Temporal Units

`Five beats of deliberately unequal length. The core holds 9 of the 30 seconds (30%); a whole day passes in 5.`

## Temporal Sequence

| Time | Beat | Content | Density |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[0:00–0:05]` | 朝の教室・遅れた笑い | Morning light lays long thin shadows across the desks. 美月, close, in the next seat: 「なんか、雰囲気変わった？」 真白's face — then the smile, **arriving late**. The delay is the whole beat | SPARSE — one held face |
| `[0:05–0:09]` | 他人の指 | 美月's thumb strokes her own screen to open the app. 真白's eyes stay on the finger, too long. 「ありがとうって、送った？」 | TRANSITION — quick, one insert |
| `[0:09–0:18]` | **核**：届いてしまった言葉 | 真白 opens the thread. Slow dolly to the sent bubble: **午前3:00 ／ ありがとう、いつもごめんね。 ／ 送信済み**. Cut to her hand in her lap, closing, nails whitening. 「これ、私じゃない」— voice shaking. Then 美月's face, and 「……嬉しい」, unguarded and real. **HOLD on that smile** | **DENSE → long HOLD** |
| `[0:18–0:23]` | 一日が過ぎる | The same screen opened and closed: 昼休み, 放課後, evening. Different light each time, same unmoving 送信済み. Three quick repeats | RAPID — repetition |
| `[0:23–0:30]` | 枕の横・待つ | The hand lowers the phone **beside** the pillow — a half-beat where it hovers over the gap *under* the pillow first, then does not go there. Dark ceiling. The clock reaches 2:00. The screen lights: **おまえの代わりに、届けたよ。** Her finger trembles. She looks past the screen into the dark — nothing. 「……誰、あなた」 Cut to black | Slow → hook |

### Timing Policy

- The late laugh at `[0:03]` must be **visibly** late — roughly half a second of nothing before the mouth moves
- The hold on 美月's smile runs to the end of the core beat; do not cut off it early
- The hand's hesitation over the space under the pillow at `[0:24]` is the episode's thesis and must be legible as a *choice not taken*

### Temporal Density

`Sparse at both ends, dense in the middle. Unlike ep.1 the core is not a reveal but a face — the camera stops moving when 美月 smiles and stays stopped.`

# 9. ACTION

- `A1` 美月 leans in, head tilted, asking. 真白's face is still, then smiles — **late**
- `A2` 美月's thumb strokes her screen open — the same practiced arc 真白's own thumb makes
- `A3` 真白's thumb opens her thread; the motion is ordinary until it reaches the bubble, then slows
- `A4` 真白's free hand closes in her lap, out of frame of the conversation. Nails press into the palm. **This is the reaction; the face does not perform one**
- `A5` 美月 smiles — wide, easy, genuinely pleased. Eyes narrowing, corner of the mouth lifting first
- `A6` The open-and-close of the phone, three times, in three different lights
- `A7` The hand carries the phone toward the pillow, **hesitates over the gap beneath it**, then sets it down beside instead
- `A8` 真白 lies back, arms at her sides, eyes open, not touching the phone
- `A9` At the message, one finger lifts and trembles — the only movement in the frame
- `A10` She raises her eyes **past** the screen, into the dark air above it, where nothing is

# 10. CAMERA

## Camera Language

`Close and level, at desk height and futon height. Longish lens, shallow depth of field. The camera moves only twice — once into the bubble, once into the final screen. Everything else is locked or a rack focus.`

| Time | Movement |

| `[0:05–0:07]` | Insert: 美月's thumb on glass, macro-close, only the fingertip sharp |
| `[0:07–0:09]` | Rack focus from the finger up to 真白's eyes, still watching it |

# 11. MOTION

- Subject Motion: `Limited animation, twos and threes. Bodies hold; fingers move. 真白's late smile is a two-stage hold — nothing, then the expression arrives whole`
- Object Motion: `The phone has weight. It is set down, not dropped. The screen changes only by ordinary UI transitions`
- Environmental Motion: `Classroom: out-of-focus students, a rolling pen, papers being handed back. Night: nothing moves at all`
- Physical Characteristics: `Ordinary inertia everywhere. The one trembling finger at [0:29] is small and involuntary, not a shake`

# 12. EMOTION

## Emotional Arc

`Guardedness → unease → the specific ache of being helped by something you did not consent to → the decision to wait for it`

## Emotional Events

| Time | Emotion | Expressed by |
|---|---|---|
| `[0:03]` | Concealment | The half-second gap before the smile |
| `[0:13]` | Fear | The hand closing in her lap. Not the face |
| `[0:16]` | **The ache** | 美月's happiness — the warmer it is, the worse it is |
| `[0:21]` | Compulsion | Opening the same screen a third time |
| `[0:25]` | Resolve | The hand choosing *beside* over *under* |
| `[0:29]` | Address | Looking past the screen at nothing, and speaking to it |

> **要**：この話の痛みは、美月が**本気で嬉しい**ことから生まれる。美月に不審・不気味・含みを持たせた瞬間、話が壊れる。

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST

- 美月's happiness at `[0:16]` is **genuine and warm**. She is not suspicious, not uneasy, not hiding anything
- The sent bubble renders exactly: **ありがとう、いつもごめんね。** with **送信済み** beneath it and the timestamp **午前3:00**
- The final message renders exactly: **おまえの代わりに、届けたよ。**
- The smile at `[0:03]` arrives **late**
- The hand **hesitates over the space under the pillow** before setting the phone beside it
- The episode ends on 「……誰、あなた」 and cuts to black

## MUST NOT

- **Do not show the ghost.** ニジ has not appeared yet and does not appear in this episode — no figure, no face, no silhouette, no eyes, no reflection, no second person anywhere
- **No rainbow, no iridescence, no colored afterimage.** Her color is introduced in ep.3; showing it here destroys her arrival
- No voice for the ghost. It exists only as text in a bubble
- Do not make 美月 sinister, doubtful, or knowing
- Do not have 真白 cry, gasp, or widen her eyes
- No scene outside the classroom, the corridor, and the bedroom
- No horror grammar: no sting, no distortion, no flicker, no wind

## PREFER

- The hand in the lap over the face, every time a reaction is needed
- Light doing the work of time passing
- Silence at the emotional peak

## ALLOW

- Slight variation in classroom background students between cuts
- The exact framing of the three repeats to differ a little, as long as the screen is matched

# 20. ITERATION

## Version

`v1 — untested`

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **The model adds a ghost.** The strongest failure mode in this episode, because the premise invites it. Check every frame of the night beats for a figure, a face, a reflection, or a rainbow
2. **美月 reads as sinister.** If her smile is ambiguous, the episode inverts into horror and loses its subject. Regenerate on the Visual and Master slots
3. **The late smile lands on time.** If the delay is not visible, the opening beat has no content
4. **The pillow hesitation reads as fumbling** rather than as a choice. If so, lengthen the hover and hold the frame
5. **Japanese text legibility** — as in ep.1, if the bubbles are unreadable, generate the screens as plates and composite

## Next Generation

`If the ghost appears at all, move the no-ghost clause to the front of the Master Prompt as well as the Negative, and remove the word "ghost" from every slot — the model is picking it up as an instruction rather than a prohibition.`
