# 5. OBJECTS（この話固有）

## Object

- ID: `FACEDOWN_PHONE`
- Name: `伏せたスマホ`
- Type: `The episode's whole thesis, as an object`
- Appearance: `The phone lying face-down on the desk, its plain black back upward. Conspicuous precisely because it is trying not to be. Later, at 2:00, light escapes from beneath its edges and lies on the desk around it`
- Function: `Her attempt to stop depositing time — and the proof that it did not work`

### Importance

# 7. NARRATIVE

## Core Event

She puts the phone down for a week — and the log grows anyway, naming hours she spent with actual people, in a column where an app's name should be.

## Beginning

A week of the face-down phone. The hand that reaches across the desk and finds nothing.

## Development

Morning. She opens the screen-time notification for a day on which she never touched the screen.

## Turning Point

The app column reads 会話 and 部活. Not apps. Lunch. Club. The receipt was never about the screen.

## Climax

2:00. The face-down phone lights **from underneath**. ニジ, on the screen: 「おまえが誰かに預けた、時間の領収書」

## Ending

「どうせ」 — ニジ says the word before 真白 has finished thinking it. 「その言葉が、いちばん、あぶないんだよ」…「減らしても、消えないよ。だって、預けたんだもの」

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

## Temporal Units

`Five beats. Unusually, the fastest beat comes FIRST — a week compressed into seven seconds — because the episode's argument requires the week to be felt as effort before it is revealed as futile.`

## Temporal Sequence

| Time | Beat | Content | Density |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[0:00–0:07]` | 一週間 | Repetition, fast: the phone face-down on the desk, black back up, too conspicuous. Her hand crossing the empty desk, searching, finding nothing. A station platform, her fist closed inside her pocket. The face-down phone buzzing **once** at lunch — and not being turned over. Light changing across the desk to mark days passing | **RAPID — a week in 7s** |
| `[0:07–0:12]` | 朝、通知を開く | Morning. She finally picks it up and opens the screen-time notification for yesterday — a day she never touched | TRANSITION |
| `[0:12–0:21]` | **核**：アプリでない名前 | Slow dolly onto the panel. **午前12時14分〜午前12時39分 ／ 使用時間　25分 ／ アプリ　　会話**. Then **午後4時05分〜午後4時47分 ／ 使用時間　42分 ／ アプリ　　部活**. Two half-second inserts of what those hours actually were: 美月's face mid-sentence at lunch; a senior's hands over a clipboard after school — both **without a phone anywhere in frame**. Back to the panel. Hold. 「……どうして」 | **DENSE → HOLD** |
| `[0:21–0:26]` | 下から光る | 2:00. The face-down phone **lights from beneath its own edges**, laying a thin glow on the desk around it. She turns it over. ニジ is sitting on the screen, and the iridescence bleeds softly a little way into the dark air of the room. 「久しぶり。おまえ、一週間も来なかったから、ちょっと淋しかったよ」 | Slow, warm |
| `[0:26–0:30]` | どうせ | 「どうせ」— ニジ says it *first*, in 真白's own cadence. 真白's mouth is still half-open on the unspoken word. 「その言葉が、いちばん、あぶないんだよ」…「減らしても、消えないよ。だって、預けたんだもの」 Cut to black | Hook |

### Timing Policy

- The week beat must read as **effort**, not as a stylish montage — each fragment is a small refusal
- The two life-inserts at `[0:16]` and `[0:18]` are half a second each and contain **no phone**. That absence is the point
- At `[0:26]`, ニジ's 「どうせ」 must land **before** 真白 speaks — her lips are already shaped for it

### Temporal Density

`Fast → slow → slowest → warm → hook. The inversion of the usual shape: this episode spends its opening on time she tried to save.`

# 11. MOTION

- Subject Motion: `Limited animation, twos and threes. During the week beat 真白's body is almost inert; only the searching hand moves. ニジ, when she arrives, is loose and warm`
- Object Motion: `The face-down phone buzzes once — a short, dull, weighted rattle against the desk, not a slide. It does not move by itself otherwise`
- Environmental Motion: `Changing daylight across the desk is the only motion in the first seven seconds`
- Physical Characteristics: `Everything has ordinary weight. The iridescence bleeding into the room is a soft haze, not a projection or a beam`

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST

- The panel renders exactly, including the double-width spacing of the source layout: **午前12時14分〜午前12時39分 ／ 使用時間　25分 ／ アプリ　　会話**, and **午後4時05分〜午後4時47分 ／ 使用時間　42分 ／ アプリ　　部活**
- The two life-inserts contain **no phone anywhere in frame**
- The face-down phone lights **from underneath** before it is turned over
- ニジ says 「どうせ」 **before** 真白 speaks it
- ニジ is **fully opaque**

## MUST NOT

- **ニジ must not be translucent or fading.** Transparency begins in ep.8
- **ニジ must not say 「わたし」**
- ニジ must not step out of the phone or stand in the room at human scale; the iridescence may bleed a little into the dark air, but she stays in the screen
- Do not show 真白 using the phone during the week beat — not once
- Do not explain the mechanism visually (no diagrams, no glowing threads between people, no time-transfer imagery)
- No horror grammar: no sting, no distortion, no flicker, no wind
- The senior in the club insert is **not** 湊 and must not be framed as anyone in particular — 湊's introduction is ep.6

## PREFER

- Absence over presence — the empty desk, the phone-free inserts
- Light doing the work of the week passing

## ALLOW

- Slight variation in the desk framing across the week beat
- The iridescence to bleed softly a short way past the glass into the dark

# 20. ITERATION

## Version

`v1 — untested`

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **The panel's app column renders as an app name** (a messaging icon, "LINE", etc.) instead of 会話 / 部活. This is the episode's entire reveal — check it character by character
2. **A phone sneaks into the life-inserts.** Models put phones in classroom scenes by default. If one appears, regenerate with "no phone visible" moved into the Master slot
3. **The week beat becomes a stylish montage** and loses the sense of effort. If so, slow the cuts and hold each empty-desk frame longer
4. **The under-lighting reads as a glitch effect.** It should look like an ordinary screen glowing under an ordinary object, just at an impossible time
5. **ニジ renders translucent** — the standing risk for every episode from here to ep.7

## Next Generation

`If 会話 / 部活 will not render, generate the panel as a plate and composite. The rest of the episode survives without it; the reveal does not.`
