# 5. OBJECTS（この話固有）

## Object

- ID: `NIJI_FORM`
- Name: `虹色の残像 → ニジ`
- Type: `The apparition, inside the screen`
- Appearance: `A smeared iridescent afterimage on the dark screen that resolves, as she narrows her eyes, into an outline: shoulder-length hair, a thin neck — and then 真白's own face, one step younger. Longer lashes. Slightly fuller cheeks. Fully opaque. The colors drift slowly at the edge: blue → green → blue`
- Material: `Screen light. A residue, not a projection — no rays, no particles, no aura`
- Function: `The story's second character, arriving for the first time`

### Importance

- Narrative Importance: `CRITICAL`
- Visual Importance: `CRITICAL`
- Continuity Importance: `CRITICAL`

> **開示の位置**：この話でニジは**完全に不透明**である。透け始めるのは第8話（指先だけ）、はっきり薄くなるのは第9話。ここで少しでも透かせば、後半の「返すたびに薄くなる」という代償の構造が無効になる。

# 7. NARRATIVE

## Core Event

The thing in the phone has no name, and asks its victim to give it one. She does. It smiles as if it is about to cry.

## Beginning

2:00 A.M. The screen brightens **by itself** — not a notification, not a call. The dark in the room is pushed back a little.

## Development

真白 takes the phone in both hands. A smeared rainbow inside the glass gathers into an outline: her own face, one step younger. 「やっほー」 It tilts its head — and the tilt is exactly her own.

## Turning Point

It explains, lightly, that it is made of time she handed to other people — likes, read receipts, replies. Then: 「名前、ないの？」「ないよ。おまえが、つけて」

## Climax

The rainbow drifts blue → green → blue at the edge of the screen. 「……ニジ」 It repeats the name, and **smiles as though about to cry**.

## Ending

「返してくれたら、帰れるんだよ」 …「でも、おまえ、まだ、返せないんでしょ」 It waves. The colors go out one by one; the last single hue holds a moment, then goes. The dark glass gives 真白 back her own face.

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

## Temporal Units

`Five beats. The naming holds 8 of the 30 seconds. The arrival — structurally the largest visual event in the series — is given 7 and no more, because the episode's subject is not the arrival but what the arrival asks for.`

## Temporal Sequence

| Time | Beat | Content | Density |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[0:00–0:05]` | ひとりでに光る | 2:00. The dark room. The screen brightens **slowly and on its own** — no notification banner, no ring. The darkness visibly retreats from it. 真白 sits up and takes the phone in **both hands** | SPARSE — dread, no event |
| `[0:05–0:12]` | **到着** | Inside the glass: a smeared iridescent blur. She narrows her eyes and it resolves — shoulder-length hair, thin neck, then a face. **Her own face, one step younger.** 「やっほー」 She cannot speak. 「……だれ」 Her fingers grip the futon and whiten. It **tilts its head** — her own tilt, exactly — and that, not the face, is the moment of horror | DENSE — the reveal |
| `[0:12–0:16]` | 説明は速く | 「おまえが誰かに預けた時間。それが、集まると、こういうのができるんだよ」「いいね、とか、既読、とか、返事、とか。そういうの、ぜんぶ時間だよ」— delivered lightly, almost cheerfully, at speed | RAPID — exposition compressed |
| `[0:16–0:24]` | **核**：名づけ | 「名前、ないの？」「ないよ。おまえが、つけて」「……勝手に、つけていいの」「いいよ。だって、おまえのものだもん」 The colors at the screen's edge drift blue → green → blue. 真白: 「……ニジ」「虹色してるから。……ニジ」 It repeats the name — 「ニジ、ね。……いい名前」 — and **smiles as if about to cry**. **HOLD** | **DENSE → long HOLD** |
| `[0:24–0:30]` | 帰れる／まだ返せない | 「どうしたら、消えるの」「消える、じゃなくて、——返してくれたら、帰れるんだよ」 …「でも、おまえ、まだ、返せないんでしょ」 It waves. The iridescence goes out color by color; **one last hue holds**, then goes. The dark glass reflects 真白's own face. Cut to black | Slow → hook |

### Timing Policy

- The brightening in `[0:00–0:05]` must be **slow enough to be uncanny** — two to three seconds of gradual increase, not a snap
- The resolve at `[0:07–0:09]` should be a **focus/clarity change**, not a morph or a materialization effect
- The head tilt at `[0:11]` gets its own held frame. It is the horror beat
- The hold on the almost-crying smile runs to `[0:24]` and is not cut short
- The final color winking out at `[0:29]` is one hue alone, held for a beat before it goes

### Temporal Density

`Slow at both ends, with two dense clusters — the arrival and the naming — separated by the fastest four seconds in the episode. The exposition is deliberately the least weighted thing in it.`

# 9. ACTION

- `A1` The screen brightens by itself; nothing else in the room changes
- `A2` 真白 sits up slowly and lifts the phone with **both hands** — she has never held it this way before
- `A3` She narrows her eyes; the blur resolves
- `A4` Her free hand grips the futon; the knuckles and nail beds whiten
- `A5` ニジ waves once, small and casual: 「やっほー」
- `A6` **ニジ tilts her head** — the exact angle 真白 makes in a mirror
- `A7` ニジ talks with her hands a little, light and unguarded, while explaining
- `A8` The iridescence at the screen's edge shifts blue → green → blue, slowly
- `A9` ニジ repeats the name to herself, then smiles — the mouth lifting while the eyes do the opposite
- `A10` ニジ waves goodbye; the colors extinguish one at a time
- `A11` The dark glass returns 真白's reflection; she does not move

# 11. MOTION

- Subject Motion: `Limited animation, twos and threes. 真白 barely moves at all — she is holding still the way people hold still around an animal. ニジ moves more, and more freely, than 真白 ever does: this contrast is the character design`
- Object Motion: `The phone is held in two hands and does not waver. It never moves by itself, never glitches, never distorts`
- Environmental Motion: `Nothing. No curtain, no wind, no shadows`
- Physical Characteristics: `The iridescence has no physics — it is a residue in the glass, drifting like a stain in water, never like fire, smoke, or particles`

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST

- ニジ is **真白's own face, one step younger** — longer lashes, slightly fuller cheeks, same hair length, same features otherwise
- ニジ is **fully opaque**. Solid. Not translucent, not ghostly, not glowing through
- ニジ exists **inside the screen** and nowhere else
- The head tilt at `[0:11]` matches 真白's own
- ニジ **smiles as if about to cry** when she receives the name — and does not cry
- The screen brightens **by itself**, slowly, with no notification banner and no sound
- The last color goes out **alone and last**, after the others
- The episode ends on the dark glass reflecting 真白's face

## MUST NOT

- **ニジ must not be translucent, transparent, or fading.** She is at full opacity in this episode. Transparency begins in ep.8 and is the story's cost mechanic — spending it here destroys it
- **ニジ must not say 「わたし」.** Her first use of the pronoun is the climax of ep.7
- **ニジ must not be a different character design.** Not a generic anime ghost, not a spirit girl, not a doll, not a mascot — she is the protagonist's own face
- **ニジ must not stand in the room.** No full-body figure at human scale, no stepping out of the phone, no floating in the bedroom
- The iridescence is a **smeared afterimage in the glass** — no rays, no light beams, no particles, no sparkles, no aura, no lens flare
- ニジ does not cry. No tears
- No horror grammar: no jump scare, no sting, no distortion, no flicker, no wind, no moving shadows
- Do not reveal what 「宛先」 means or name anyone. The answer is 「おまえが、いちばんよく知ってるよ」 and stays that way

## PREFER

- Focus resolving over any materialization effect
- Mannerism over expression, for the horror
- Silence at the naming

## ALLOW

- A faint iridescent cast on 真白's face from the screen — this is the first colored light in the series and it belongs here
- ニジ to move more freely and expressively than 真白 does

# 20. ITERATION

## Version

`v1 — untested`

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **ニジ renders as a generic anime ghost girl** rather than as the protagonist's own younger face. The single most likely failure. If it happens, put the identity clause first in Master *and* Visual, and describe her by reference to the protagonist ("the same girl, one step younger") rather than by attributes
2. **She renders translucent.** Models associate "ghost" with transparency. The Negative carries `no transparent figure, no translucent body, no see-through character` for exactly this reason — check it survived
3. **She steps out of the phone.** Check she stays inside the screen in every frame
4. **The iridescence becomes VFX** — beams, sparkles, particles. If so, replace "rainbow" with "iridescent smear, like oil on water, inside the glass"
5. **The head tilt is lost.** It carries the whole horror beat; if it does not read, extend it to a full held second

## Changes

`This episode deliberately ends two seconds after its hook line, on 真白's reflection in the dark glass. The format card's rule is to cut on the hook and add nothing; the reflection is not an addition but the hook's image — she is alone with herself, having returned nothing. Recorded here as an intentional variance.`

## Next Generation

`If the identity fails twice, generate the apparition as a separate plate from a still of the protagonist's own face and composite it into the screen, rather than asking one pass to hold both faces.`
