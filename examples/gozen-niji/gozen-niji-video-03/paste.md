### B3. Motion

===== B3. MOTION START =====
Limited animation, shot on twos and threes — holds punctuated by small precise movements, never continuous interpolation. The girl in the room barely moves at all; she is holding still the way people hold still near an animal. The figure inside the screen moves more freely and more expressively than the girl ever does, and that contrast is the point. The screen's brightening at the start is slow and gradual over two to three seconds, never a snap. The figure's arrival is a change of FOCUS from blur to sharp, not a morph, not a materialization, not an assembly from particles. Her free hand grips the futon and the knuckles whiten. The figure waves small and casual, talks a little with its hands, and tilts its head once at a precise held angle. The iridescence drifts like a stain spreading in water — never like fire, smoke, sparks or flame. At the end the colors extinguish one at a time, with one last hue held alone for a beat before it goes. The phone never moves by itself and never glitches, flickers or distorts. The wall clock's second hand advances in discrete ticks. Nothing else in the room moves — no curtain, no wind, no shifting shadows. No impacts, no motion blur smears, no squash and stretch.

## 生成したら最初に見る5点

1. **ニジが透けていないか** — **最悪の失敗**。この話のニジは完全に不透明。薄くなるのは第8話以降で、それが物語の代償
2. **ニジが真白と同じ顔か** — 一般的な「アニメの幽霊少女」になっていたら作り直す。彼女は主人公自身の顔の一歩手前

4. **首のかしげ方** — 0:11。恐怖はここ一箇所だけで、所作で果たされる。読み取れなければ持続を1秒に伸ばす
