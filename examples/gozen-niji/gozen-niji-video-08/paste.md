### B3. Motion

===== B3. MOTION START =====
Limited animation, shot on twos and threes — holds punctuated by small precise movements, never continuous interpolation. The figure's movements here are the smallest and most complete of any episode; it has never been this present. It raises its hand in front of its own face and studies it calmly, the way someone checks whether something is dirty, then lowers it and keeps talking WITHOUT REACTING TO IT. The girl does not react to it either; her eyes stay on its face, not on its hand. The transparency has no physics at all — no shimmer, no flicker, no pulsing, no fading in and out; it is a constant quiet fact of how the fingers render. The iridescence trembles very slightly once, near the end, its first instability in the whole story. At the close the girl opens a thread and scrolls to the top of a very long message, and her fingertip descends, makes contact with the glass, and STOPS — motionless and held, not pressing, not tapping, not sending. Ordinary weight and inertia. The phone never moves by itself and never glitches, flickers or distorts; screens change only by ordinary UI transitions. Nothing else in the room moves. No impacts, no motion blur smears, no squash and stretch.
===== B3. MOTION END =====

===== B4. CAMERA START =====

No horror strings, no sting, no emotional swell.
