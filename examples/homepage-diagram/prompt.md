## 内容（Content）
木→板→家具の工程。伐採→製材→手仕事→蜜蝋仕上げ→届くまで。一枚の木の一貫した流れ。

## フォーマット（Format）
工程の並びを等角タイルに、一つの木目線が全工程を貫く。

## 様式（Style）
アイソメトリック：30° グリッド、消失点なし、主役に唯一の飽和色。

## 合成プロンプト（Merged）
An isometric diagram of the craft journey from forest to furniture. On a 30-degree grid: a standing cedar, its trunk sawn into a plank, the plank planed into a chair, the chair finished with beeswax, arriving in a warm room. Each stage on its own isometric tile, one grain line running unbroken through all five stages; the chair in the final tile carrying the one saturated accent (cedar-brown) against pastel tiles, denser linework around it so it reads as the goal, clean crisp edges, no perspective vanishing point. Not photorealistic, no blur.
